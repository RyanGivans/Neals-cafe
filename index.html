The Neal’s Story

A landmark built on comfort, consistency, and return visits.

Neal’s Cafe has been part of Springdale since 1944, serving the kind of home-style meals that turn a restaurant into a ritual. It is the kind of place people remember, revisit, and recommend with no hesitation.

The draw is not just the food. It is the rhythm of a real diner: familiar faces, warm rolls, breakfast early, pie later, and a room that feels lived in instead of manufactured. That is what gives Neal’s its staying power.

For longtime locals, first-timers, church groups, family lunches, and weekday regulars, the experience lands the same way: generous, grounded, and unmistakably Arkansas.

Family-owned warmth
